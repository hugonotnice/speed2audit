Decoupled Multi-Agent Topology

How the Autonomous Engine Works

Step 1

🌐 Scraper Agent

Crawls the company website to extract value propositions, catalog pricing, and ICP profile.

Step 2

🎭 Persona Generator

Creates a tailored lead profile powered by Gemini 3.6 Flash, with full Human-in-the-Loop review.

Step 3

📱 Shopper Agent

Conducts the WhatsApp chat via WAHA with realistic 15-40s humanization delays and typing presence.

Step 4

⚖️ Auditor Judge

Analyzes complete conversation logs and outputs a structured diagnostic scorecard (0-10 scale).
