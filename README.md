## Overview

**Speed2Audit** is an open-core mystery shopper and auditing platform for text and voice customer service channels.

It automates outbound mystery shopping simulations and evaluates customer support teams across critical performance metrics:
- ⏱️ **Speed to Lead & Response Times**: Measures first-response latency and ongoing turnaround times across channels.
- 🎯 **Answer Quality & Accuracy**: Evaluates clarity, policy compliance, and accuracy of customer service agents.
- 🛡️ **Objection Handling**: Tests how effectively agents manage customer friction, edge cases, and pushbacks.
- 📑 **Quote Delivery Speed**: Benchmarks the end-to-end turnaround time for pricing and quote delivery.
